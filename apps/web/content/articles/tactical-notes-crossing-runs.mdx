# Tactical Notes: The Crossing Movement

The cross-and-finish play has survived every meta shift eFootball has thrown
at us. Three things keep it viable:

1. **Aerial dominance** — centre-forwards with 90+ Heading + Jump win the
   long-ball second-ball battle even when outmuscled.
2. **Wide delivery** — inverted wingers with the `EarlyCross` playstyle get
   the ball in with enough whip to beat backtracking full-backs.
3. **Compact recovery** — once the ball is lost out wide, your midfield
   can collapse centrally without losing shape.

## Build Template

Pair an AMF with a `Chip Shot Control` skill alongside a CF with `Heading`
above 90. The AMF drives the wide receiver, who has the option to deliver
early or carry to the byline. If the cross is blocked, recycled possession
leads to a half-space shot.

> The result is a play that scores *and* keeps the ball if it fails — the
> definition of a meta-stable tactic.

Watch for: defenders with `Man Mark` draining your AMF. Swap to a `Double
Touch` winger as a counter-counter.
