# Fabian Schär Carries: Sep '26 Card Reviewed

The September 2026 Show Time drops include a Fabian Schär that pushes CB
meta in a refreshing direction. Here's the breakdown:

## Headline Stats

- **Defensive Awareness** 92
- **Ball Winning** 91
- **Jump** 88
- **Heading** 87
- **Aggression** 84

These give you a tackle-first CB who recovers well in tight spaces. The 88
Jump pushes him over the line for contested long balls.

## Why The Card Matters

A few notes from training:

- Pairing with a sweeper-style CB (high Defensive Engagement) unlocks the
  +2 Aerial booster without sacrificing ground defense.
- His Weak Foot Accuracy is 4 — clean enough for short transitions.
- His Trickster off-ball trait is neutral for a CB.

## Verdict

A solid 93-overall anchor for any 4-ATB back line. The Schär card is the
safest "Buy" of the September pack window — he'll start in your XI this
season and stay there next.

Skip if: you run a 3-ATB and already rely on a Wide CB with the
`DefensiveEngagement` boost. Schär doesn't carry the side channels well.
