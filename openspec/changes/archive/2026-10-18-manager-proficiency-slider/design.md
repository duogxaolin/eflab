## Context

See proposal.md. The engine computes training, then boosters, then the estimated overall from fitted per-position weights. Evidence outside the repository (six measured cards, a population check on 9,839 cards) gives a per-level rate table and shows the unanchored goalkeeper overall biased low (−0.74 on the 702 goalkeepers of the population check, within ±1 83.6 %) and the anchored form less so (−0.66, 97.4 %).

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** ship the slider, the bonus model and the anchored overall with honest limits and oracle tests that fail when the model is tampered with.
**Non-Goals:** per-manager effects, position tiles other than the registered position, Position Training, In-Match Roles.

## Decisions

1. **Bonus between training and boosters.** shown stat = s + bonus + booster 1 + booster 2, with s = base + training (Jump already receives Aerial and Goalkeeper 1). bonus = 0 for s ≥ 99, otherwise `min(99 − s, floor(s × r(level)))`; boosters may push a stat above 99.
2. **Rate table.** Levels 70–76 → 0; 77 → 0.02116; 78 → 0.02286; 79–80 → 0.02614; 81–82 → 0.02878; 83 → 0.03031; 84–85 → 0.03243; 87 → 0.03419; 88 → 0.0355; 89 → 0.03593; 90 → 0.03625. Level 86 has no single rate that fits; it uses `floor((s + 6) / 32)`, marked as an unconfirmed exception in code, docs and UI. The cells s = 66 at level 83 and s = 88 at level 87 stay ambiguous and are excluded from the fixture.
3. **Anchor.** anchor = card overall − projected overall at the registered position from the card's base stats without boosters; displayed overall = round(projected overall of the shown stats + anchor). Used only at the registered position; other positions keep the fitted proficiency penalty and show a warning worded by proficiency (see Results, off-position tiles). The anchor was chosen post hoc (see Risks).
4. **Default 90.** The slider starts at 90 like efootbase, which raises the opening overall by about 4 compared with the old no-bonus value; Reset keeps the current proficiency and only clears training.
5. **Optimal allocation (decided in the verify round).** The exact dynamic programme stays linear (it ignores the bonus floors and the 99 cap), so the preset does not depend on the proficiency level. Reason: efootbase's own Max OVR build is one allocation per card across every level it was read at (5/5 cards with a recorded Max build, 89 readings), i.e. efootbase does not optimise with the bonus either. A bonus-aware exact optimum (grouped knapsack; Aerial Strength and Goalkeeper 1 form one joint group because both lift Jump) was implemented in scratch and measured: it moves Messi's build off efootbase's 8/4/12/8/7 at 80, 85 and 90 (e.g. 9/4/9/8/8/Aerial 4 at 90) and does not improve the match with efootbase's Max readings (89/89 within ±1 either way; exact 67 vs 65). Cost of keeping the linear preset, measured on the 261 hub-oracle cards × 21 levels at the registered position with both boosters and the anchor: the bonus-aware optimum is higher on 1,694/5,481 pairs, by at most 0.668 before rounding and by 1 displayed point on 202 pairs (never more), and equal at 70–76. (The verifier's local search had found 69/261 better and 7/261 +1 displayed at 90; the exact search finds 156/261 and 14/261 at 90.) The "Max OVR Preset" requirement is reworded to that bound and test section 8 asserts it with the exact search as an independent reference.
6. **Fixture.** Numbers only (card and position indices, base stats, booster vectors, allocations, level, displayed stats and overall), at most 300 lines, built from the proposal file in the scratch area; no names or third-party identifiers.

## Results (implementation, 2026-10-18)

The evidence is kept in four separate groups; only the second and third predict data the rates were not fitted on.

- **In-sample fixture** (`__fixtures__/proficiency-oracle.json`, rates pooled over all six cards — the fixture is the fitting data, so the tests are a regression and tamper guard, not a validation): 280 lines, 6 cards, 263 builds, 6,838 stat cells — 100 % exact; anchored overall within ±1 263/263 (goalkeepers 71/71), unanchored 218/263 (goalkeepers 26/71). No ambiguous cell is in the selection. Each level's feasible rate interval is re-derived from the fixture; every shipped rate lies inside it and the level-86 interval is empty (its exception reproduces 207/207 cells).
- **Held-out** (rates read off one outfield card, Messi SS at 70/75/80/85/90, and one goalkeeper, Neuer at 70/90, then frozen; Cannavaro CB, Čech GK, Rodri DMF and Haaland CF predicted at 70/75/80/85/90): 178 builds, 4,628/4,628 stat cells exact (also at the low and high ends of the two-card intervals); overall within ±1 unanchored 142/178, anchored 178/178. The anchor changes the result only on Čech (14/50 → 50/50) — the card whose failures led to the anchor (post hoc); Cannavaro, Rodri and Haaland pass unanchored too, so this run does not test the anchor.
- **Prospective** (rates from Messi, Neuer, Cannavaro and Čech; Rodri and Haaland predicted at all 21 levels, 164 builds): stat cells 4,263/4,264 at the interval lower bounds, 4,257/4,264 at the midpoints, 4,252/4,264 just under the upper bounds (misses at 79, 81, 82, 83, where the four-card intervals are wide); overall within ±1 164/164 anchored and unanchored. The shipped rates at 79/81/82/83 were narrowed by Rodri and Haaland and are in-sample for them.
- **Not covered by a two-card held-out run**: the 15 levels between the held-out ones (71–74, 76–79, 81–84, 87–89) were measured on four cards only; the 100 % fit there needs Rodri + Haaland in the pool. Level 86's exception was fitted on all six cards (no held-out) and is non-monotone in the level (s = 26–29 and s = 58 gain 1 more at 86 than at 87). Each shipped rate is one point inside its measured six-card interval (widths 0.00022–0.00092); tamper runs showed that rates moved inside the interval (r90 = 0.0362, r80 = 0.0262, r83 = 0.0300) pass every test, which is by design — the data cannot distinguish them.
- **Population check** (anchor only, no bonus involved; formula on hub max stats + Booster 1 vs efootbase's overall at max level; run after the anchor was chosen, on independent data), within ±1 unanchored → anchored: 9,839 cards with level cap > 1 in both sources: all 97.0 % → 98.3 %, GK 83.6 % → 97.4 % (702); all 10,073 cards with dump max level > 1 (234 have hub level cap 1, so their hub "max" is their base): 94.7 % → 96.1 %, GK 82.0 % → 95.5 % (716). Anchored bias −0.33 (GK −0.66) on the 9,839. On Messi the anchored overall matches efootbase exactly on 4/8 Max/Reset readings, the unanchored on 6/8 (all within ±1).
- **Off-position tiles** (efootbase's per-position tiles of the six cards, every build, bonus applied, unanchored engine with the penalty): full-proficiency off positions within ±1 202/202 (mean −0.17); half proficiency +3.80 on average, 0/224 within ±1 (all +3 to +5); non-proficient with our estimate above the floor of 40: +2.6, 122/2,483 within ±1; non-proficient with our estimate at the floor (goalkeeper cards at outfield positions, outfield cards at GK — the verifier's first count had dropped the tiles reading 40 and the outfield-at-GK tiles): within ±1 on 1,145/1,231, 1,098 exactly 40, so no "higher" claim is made there. The panel warning is worded per proficiency (and per floor) with these numbers.
- **Regression at 70**: stats and the unanchored estimate equal the pre-change engine on 61,074/61,074 builds; with the card overall passed (as the web does) 1,336 of 4,698 registered-position builds round differently — the anchor, not the bonus.
- **Max OVR**: the linear DP picks the SS card's measured Max build exactly (Shooting 8, Passing 4, Dribbling 12, Dexterity 8, Lower Body 7); on the other four cards with a recorded Max build the allocation differs but the estimate stays within ±1 of the measured Max reading (89/89 readings in the fixture). Bound against the bonus-aware optimum: decision 5.
- **Spec correction**: the proposal's "80 → 83 at 90" scenario was arithmetically wrong (80 × 0.03625 = 2.9 → +2); the scenario now reads 80 → 82 and 83 → 86.
- **Tamper checks** (scratch copies outside the repo): changing r(80) to 0.0255, dropping the anchor, or adding the boosters before the bonus each fail the suite.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **[Risk]** The anchor was chosen post hoc, after seeing the unanchored goalkeeper failures; its held-out 178/178 is not evidence for it → Mitigation: the population check (9,839 cards, 702 goalkeepers; independent data) is cited as its support everywhere, and the docs and the panel no longer quote 178/178 as validation.
- **[Risk]** 15 intermediate rates and the level-86 exception have no two-card held-out evidence; in-interval rate changes are undetectable → Mitigation: stated in the docs; the tests guard against out-of-interval changes only.
- **[Risk]** Level 86 is a fitted, non-monotone exception → Mitigation: labelled unconfirmed everywhere it is user-visible.
- **[Trade-off]** Non-registered positions remain approximate: full-proficiency positions agree with efootbase's tiles, half / none read about 4 / 2–3 lower than efootbase — stated in the panel warning.
- **[Trade-off]** Max OVR ignores the bonus (matches efootbase's level-independent preset); a hand-made build can read up to 1 displayed point higher — stated in the panel and asserted by test.
